# Actionable Review Feedback

Re-read the affected Review Proposals and enumerate attributable feedback by permanent provider ID. Return the open ticket to unassigned `ready-for-agent` through human authority, then return to the [Resume Gate](RECOVERY.md) with Review Feedback qualified as the sole source.

Corrections reuse only still-open proposals. If feedback requires changing an already merged delivery, preserve its confirmed result and enter the human boundary for an explicit follow-up plan. Never reopen or republish that proposal as if its merge had not happened.

Carry the feedback IDs and affected deliveries into the Resume Gate. Recovery owns Authority input, evidence classification, and the Resumption Record. Resume corrections at the earliest required step in [the delivery workflow](SKILL.md#5-execute-one-flat-attempt), reusing existing open proposals. That workflow owns implementation, item dispositions, validation, and fresh reviews.

**Complete when:** attributable actionable feedback and the human-completed review round qualify as the sole Resume Gate source, with affected deliveries and existing proposals identified; otherwise the human boundary owns the unresolved condition.
